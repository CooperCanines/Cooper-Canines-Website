Clause 1. Release of Liability

The Client acknowledges that participation in dog training sessions involves inherent risks, including but not limited to: dog bites, scratches, injury to persons or property, and unpredictable animal behavior. The Client voluntarily assumes all risks associated with participation in training sessions, classes, provided materials, or related activities provided by the Trainer. The Client understands that dog behavior is inherently unpredictable and that even well-trained dogs may react unexpectedly. In consideration of the services provided, the Client agrees to release, indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Trainer from any and all claims, demands, causes of action, damages, or liabilities of any kind, whether known or unknown, arising out of or connected in any way to participation in dog training services, including but not limited to injury or damage caused by the Client's dog, other dogs, or third parties.

Clause 2. Veterinary Care

The Client certifies that their dog(s) are up to date on vaccinations as required by both state and local laws, including but not limited to rabies vaccination. The Client agrees to provide proof of vaccinations upon request and confirms their dog(s) are free of contagious conditions. The Client accepts full responsibility for any veterinary expenses resulting from injury to the Client's dog or caused by the Client's dog during training. The Trainer is authorized to seek veterinary care, if the Client or Emergency Contact cannot be reached, for the Client's dog if deemed necessary, and the Client agrees to reimburse all related costs. The Trainer reserves the right to refuse or discontinue services if a dog’s health status presents a risk.

Clause 3. Owner/Handler Responsibility

The Client is responsible for their dog’s behavior and safety. The Client agrees to provide complete and accurate disclosure of behavioral history and prior training experiences. Failure to disclose relevant behavioral history may impact safety and effectiveness and may result in suspension or termination of services. The Client also warrants that the location where training will occur is safe and secure. The Trainer is not responsible for injury, damage, or loss resulting from the conditions or layout of the premises.

The Trainer makes no claims or guarantees (expressed or implied) regarding the dog’s behavior, performance, or response to training. Specific service type, location, duration, materials, and objectives will be agreed upon verbally or in writing. The Client understands that the services provided are instructional and advisory in nature. Successful outcomes are heavily dependent upon the Client’s consistent effort, participation, and adherence to the Trainer’s instructions and protocols.

Clause 5. Trainer’s Right to Refer

The Trainer may recommend referral to another professional (such as a veterinarian or behavior specialist) if the dog’s behavioral or medical needs exceed the scope of services provided. The Client agrees to assume all responsibility for the cost of any recommended third-party services.

If any provision of this agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. Clients are encouraged to attend all scheduled training sessions. Trainer may, at their sole discretion, offer a make-up session or credit for unforeseen circumstances. Excessive cancellations or no-shows may result in suspension or termination of services.

All training packages must be used within six (6) months from the date of purchase, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Unused sessions after this period will expire without refund.
